## What is AsterIDE?

AsterIDE is a developing text editor and integrated development environment designed with a focus on flexibility, performance, and thoughtful design. What began as a simple experiment has evolved into a serious project aimed at creating a reliable and extensible tool for developers.

---

## Origins
AsterIDE began as a lighthearted idea, inspired by an inside joke shared with a friend and centered around a simple GIF. Initially, the project was not intended to become anything substantial. It served as a casual experiment and a way to explore an idea without expectations.

However, as development progressed, the project quickly became more engaging. What started as a joke turned into something worth refining and I started to enjoy it. The more time invested into building and shaping the idea, the more it became clear that AsterIDE had the potential to grow into something meaningful.

This realization led to a shift in direction. Rather than remaining an inside joke, AsterIDE became a serious development effort focused on building a decent text editor / IDE from the ground up.

## Philosophy
AsterIDE is built around a hands-on development philosophy. The goal is not only to create a functional tool, but also to deeply understand the systems and design decisions behind it.

The project emphasizes:
- Simplicity where possible
- Flexibility where necessary
- Transparency in how the system operates
- Continuous learning through building
- User Experience
- Customization and free will essentially

This approach allows AsterIDE to serve both as a practical tool and as a platform for experimentation, growth and fun :)

## Goals
The primary goal of AsterIDE is to evolve into a powerful and reliable development environment. Key objectives include:

- Building a stable and efficient text editing core
- Supporting extensibility and customization
- Providing a clean and intuitive user experience
- Enabling workflows that align with modern development practices

## Roadmap
While AsterIDE is still actively evolving, several areas of development are planned:

- Expansion of core editing features
- Performance optimizations (mostly for searching)
- Improved configuration and customization options
- Integration with development tools and workflows (for example SCM)
- Ongoing refinement of the user interface and experience

The roadmap is flexible and will continue to adapt as the project grows.

## Vision
AsterIDE is more than just an editor now. It is a long-term project that I fully intend to reflect continuous improvement, experimentation, and technical growth.

It serves as:
- A platform for testing new ideas
- A foundation for building personalized workflows
- A representation of evolving development practices

---

While AsterIDE was originally just because of a joke, it has developed into a focused and meaningful project. With continued effort and iteration, it has the potential to become a robust and decently designed development tool.

I look forward to any and all support the project may recieve :)
